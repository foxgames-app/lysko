Support

Lysko Wash is a calm washing game for children aged 2–4: wash toys with a sponge and water, collect stickers. No ads, no accounts, no data collection.

Contact us: itm.help.store@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days. Please tell us your device model and what happened.

Restore a purchase. Open the parents’ section (round button in the top-left corner, answer the simple question), then tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone, iPad) or Google account (Android) that made the purchase. The purchase also works on your other devices with the same account and, on iPhone and iPad, through Family Sharing.

Refunds. Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play, so refunds are requested there: reportaproblem.apple.com or Google Play help.
