Lysko Wash: Toddler Games 2-4 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Lysko Wash is a game for children aged 2–4 in which a little fox helps wash toys and pets. It is made by Ihor Tsaryk (“we”).

We collect no data. We do not receive any personal or non-personal information from the app: we have no server, and we do not share or sell anything. The app connects only to Google Play (purchases and ratings) and to GitHub Pages (the list of our games), as described below. It has no accounts, no third-party ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or other files on the device; it uses only its own storage.

On the device only. The app keeps a few things on the device: the sound and vibration settings, which toys have been washed and which stickers have been looked at (the sticker album), whether the one-time purchase has been made (yes/no), a copy of our games list with their icons, and the dates of the last list check and rating request. They never leave the device and are deleted when the app is removed. Vibration is used only as a gentle touch feedback and can be switched off in the parents’ section.

Purchases. Some toys are unlocked with an optional one-time purchase, offered only in the parents’ section, which is protected by an arithmetic question. Any purchase is processed entirely by Google Play (or the App Store); we do not receive your payment details. On Android the app uses Google’s Play Billing Library to talk to the Google Play app on the device; it asks Google Play to show the purchase window and whether this purchase was made. Google Play returns the purchase status and a purchase token; the app keeps the token only in memory, to confirm a new purchase to Google Play (the acknowledgement Google requires). Between launches the device keeps only the answer (yes/no), so the unlocked toys also work offline. We do not run a server and receive no order or account information. See Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and Apple’s: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Our other games. The app can show our other games: a small button with a game’s icon on the main screen and a list in the parents’ section. Both open the App Store or Google Play only after the arithmetic question. To keep the list up to date without an app update, the app downloads a public file with the list and the icons from foxgames-app.github.io (GitHub Pages), over an encrypted connection, at most one attempt a day. The request contains no identifiers, accounts, cookies or information about the child; like any internet request it reaches GitHub from the device’s IP address, which we do not receive or store. See GitHub’s privacy statement: docs.github.com.

Ratings. When a parent opens the parents’ section (after the arithmetic question), the app may ask Google Play (or the App Store) to show its standard rating window, at most once every 30 days; the store decides whether it actually appears. On Android this window is provided by Google Play’s In-App Review library. The “Rate this game” button there opens the app’s store page. A rating or review is entered only if the parent chooses to, and is handled by Google Play or the App Store under their policies (reviews are public in the store); the app itself does not receive or store it.

Children. The app is designed for young children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and the App Store Kids category rules. Since we receive no data, we collect no personal information of children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address.
