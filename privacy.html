Lysko: Toddler Games 2-4 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Lysko is a set of games for children aged 2–4, made by Ihor Tsaryk (“we”).

We collect no data. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or non-personal information. It has no accounts, no third-party ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

On the device only. The app keeps a few settings on the device (for example, whether the full game set has been bought, and the sound settings). They never leave the device and are deleted when the app is removed.

Purchases. 5 games are free. One optional one-time purchase unlocks 10 more games. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play (or the App Store); we do not receive your payment details. See Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and Apple’s: apple.com/legal/privacy. The purchase screen is in a parents’ section protected by an arithmetic question.

Our other games. The app can show our other games: a small button with a game’s icon and the words “More games” on the main screen and a list in the parents’ section. Both open the App Store or Google Play only after the arithmetic question. To keep the list up to date without an app update, the app downloads a public file with the list and the icons from foxgames-app.github.io (GitHub Pages), over an encrypted connection, at most one attempt a day. The request contains no identifiers, accounts, cookies or information about the child; like any internet request it reaches GitHub from the device’s IP address, which we do not receive or store. See GitHub’s privacy statement: docs.github.com. The list, the icons and the time of the last check are kept on the device and deleted with the app.

Children. The app is designed for young children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and the App Store Kids category rules. Since no data is collected, no personal information of children is collected.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address.

Contact: itm.help.store@gmail.com

Não coletamos dados. O app não coleta, não armazena em nenhum servidor, não compartilha nem vende nenhuma informação, pessoal ou não. Não tem contas, anúncios de terceiros, análises, rastreamento nem SDKs de terceiros. Não acessa câmera, microfone, localização, contatos nem arquivos.

Compras. 5 jogos são gratuitos. Uma compra única e opcional desbloqueia mais 10 jogos. Política de privacidade do Google: policies.google.com/privacy, da Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy. A tela de compra fica em uma área para os pais, protegida por uma pergunta de matemática.
