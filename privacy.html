Lysko: Toddler Games 2-4 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Lysko is a set of games for children aged 2–4, made by Ihor Tsaryk (“we”).

We collect no data. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or non-personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

On the device only. The app keeps a few settings on the device (for example, whether the full game set has been bought, and the sound settings). They never leave the device and are deleted when the app is removed.

Purchases. 5 games are free. One optional one-time purchase unlocks 10 more games. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play (or the App Store); we do not receive your payment details. See Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and Apple’s: apple.com/legal/privacy. The purchase screen is in a parents’ section protected by an arithmetic question.

Our other games. The app can show our other games: a small button with a game’s icon on the main screen and a list in the parents’ section. Both open the App Store or Google Play only after the arithmetic question. To keep the list up to date without an app update, the app downloads a public file with the list and the icons from foxgames-app.github.io (GitHub Pages), over an encrypted connection, at most one attempt a day. The request contains no identifiers, accounts, cookies or information about the child; like any internet request it reaches GitHub from the device’s IP address, which we do not receive or store. See GitHub’s privacy statement: docs.github.com.

Ratings. When a parent opens the parents’ section (after the arithmetic question), the app may ask Google Play or the App Store to show their standard rating window, at most once every 30 days; the store decides whether it actually appears. The “Rate this game” button there opens the app’s store page. A rating or review is entered only if the parent chooses to, and is handled by Google Play or the App Store under their policies (reviews are public in the store); the app itself does not receive or store it.

Children. The app is designed for young children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and the App Store Kids category rules. Since no data is collected, no personal information of children is collected.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address.
